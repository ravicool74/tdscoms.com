## Overview

A secure, role-based Office File, Project, Billing, and Payment Management System designed for departmental workflows, sub-division management, contractor portal access, agreement BOQ schedules, and Bihar PWD/RCD Form 26 voucher invoice generation.

### Key Features
- **7 User Roles & Access Control:** Admin, Executive Engineer (EE), Assistant Engineer (AE), Junior Engineer (JE), Divisional Accounts Officer (DAO), Accounts Clerk, and Contractor.
- **Project Master & Sub-Folders:** Manage office files, documents, and dynamic information sheets.
- **Two-Part Billing System:** Upload Agreement BOQ Excel sheets and input executed work-done quantities with automatic statutory deductions (TDS, GST TDS, Labour Cess, Royalty, Security Deposit).
- **Audit Trails & Security:** Full audit logging for bill approvals, payments, and workflow transitions.

## Local Development / Hosting

To run the system locally on your computer:
1. Double-click `start-localhost.bat` or run:
   ```powershell
   node server.js
   ```
2. Open your browser at [http://localhost:3000/](http://localhost:3000/)
